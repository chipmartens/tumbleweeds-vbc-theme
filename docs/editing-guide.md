# Editing the Tumbleweeds site: a plain guide

You do not need to know code. Everything on the site is a box you fill in.

## Change a page
1. Go to **Pages** and click the page.
2. **Hero Content** is the big photo at the top: photo, small label, heading, buttons.
3. **Page sections** is the rest of the page, top to bottom. Click a section's title bar to open it. Drag the handle on its left to move it. Click **Add a section** to add one.
4. Click **Update**.

## Things you will do most
| I want to | Go to |
|---|---|
| Change the info session date, time or place | Club settings, tab Info session. It disappears by itself after the day. |
| Turn off the yellow bar at the top | Club settings, tab Top of every page, switch off "Show the bar" |
| Change the words in the scrolling strip | Club settings, tab Scrolling strip |
| Change the email, Instagram or helpline number | Club settings, tabs Contact and social, Helpline |
| Add a coach | Coaches, Add New |
| Write a news post | Posts, Add New. Set a Featured image and a short Excerpt. |
| Change the menu links | Appearance, Menus |
| Mark something "Coming soon" | In any fact row, write Coming soon in Status tag. Clear it when the answer is ready. |

## Good to know
- Boxes that do not apply to your choice stay hidden. Pick the **Kind** or **Style** first and the right boxes appear.
- Words in the italic style at the end of a heading go in their own box ("Last words in italic"). You never type symbols.
- Photos: upload in Media, and write what the photo shows in the Alt text box.
- If you are not sure, leave a box empty. Empty boxes are simply not shown.
- Never type a phone number, date or fee that is not confirmed. Use a status tag instead.
